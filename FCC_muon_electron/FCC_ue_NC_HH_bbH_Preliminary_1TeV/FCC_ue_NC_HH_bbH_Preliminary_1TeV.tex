{\large\bfseries\color{ink} Fiducial definition}\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Offline $4b$ selection:} four massive bottom partons with $|\eta_b|<5$; there must exist two disjoint opposite-charge $b\bar b$ pairings with $110\le m_{b\bar b}\le130$ GeV for both pairs. No minimum $b$-parton $p_T$ or $\Delta R$ cut.
\end{itemize}
