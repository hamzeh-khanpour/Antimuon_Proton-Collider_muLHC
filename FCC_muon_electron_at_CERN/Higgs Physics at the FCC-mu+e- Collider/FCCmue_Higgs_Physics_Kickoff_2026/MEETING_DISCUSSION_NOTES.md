# Meeting discussion guide

## Introductory framing (Slides 1--9)

Emphasize that FCC-mue is a proposal for an asymmetric lepton collision mode complementary to the precision FCC-ee programme. The collider parameters and luminosities are estimates, not an approved operational configuration. The exact-energy MadGraph scan is distinct from the machine-like historical beam energies. One slide uses a redrawn schematic rather than the original machine-layout artwork; it is labelled accordingly.

## Inclusive Higgs and diagrams (Slides 14--26)

Richard requested the 4×2×6 polarization scan, now completed at the generator/integration level. Krzysztof asked specifically about self-coupling and separate weak-boson emission diagrams in HH amplitudes: the CERN original exported diagrams verify both, plus the HHVV contact vertex. The full amplitude includes interference. CC slopes ≈1 and NC slopes ≈0.22 are chiral-current consistency checks, not experimental precision on electroweak parameters.

## Four-bottom baselines (Slides 27--35)

Keep processes and final states separate. The historical samples used a nonstandard Higgs branching fraction, making naïve yield comparisons inappropriate. The complete NC4b fiducial result is under investigation because it is smaller than the separately generated bbH sample; don't explain it away without further runs. Muon collider ZH is a distinct final-state topology, calculated with the wider 80--130 GeV mass window.

## Decisions (Slides 36--40)

Agree common param cards / benchmark tables; prioritize a physics-motivated kappa3–kappa2V scan and mHH shapes; decide which detector and forward acceptance study is realistic; run initial CC/NC HHH SM feasibility checks if priorities allow; allocate roles collaboratively; set first shared paper milestone.
