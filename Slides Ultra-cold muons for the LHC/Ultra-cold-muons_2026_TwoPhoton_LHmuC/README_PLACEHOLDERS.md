# Figure placeholders

The final 26-slide deck contains one deliberate figure placeholder.

## Slide 25 - What does two-photon physics require from the LHmuC experiment?

**Purpose:** Connect the gamma-gamma physics case to the detector and interaction-region requirements discussed elsewhere in the workshop.

**Recommended figure:** A clean LHmuC / LHuC detector or interaction-region schematic that visibly includes:

- the central detector;
- the incoming and scattered muon trajectory / acceptance;
- the proton beam direction;
- possible near-beam / forward-proton instrumentation;
- optionally the location of the interaction point and any relevant nozzle / shielding element.

**Preferred source:** Laurent Forthomme's workshop contribution `Main detector: LHeC vs LHuC`, or another HELP4CERN detector concept approved by the collaboration.

**Why a placeholder is retained:** The supplied ZIP and reference PDFs did not contain a detector image that can be safely reused as the definitive LHmuC detector concept. The placeholder avoids inserting a speculative or unrelated image.
